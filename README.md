# Decoding Customer Value: A Data-Driven Retention Strategy

**Context & Affiliation**: Consulting & Analytics Club, IIT Guwahati (Summer Projects 2026)  
**Domain**: Customer Analytics, SQL Behavioral Segmentation, Retention Strategy & Founder Dashboards  
**Dataset**: 3,900 retail customers across 50 US states with transactional, promotional, and category purchasing histories.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
The brand had customer data but lacked intelligence to separate organic repeat buyers from promo-dependent bargain hunters. The goal was to engineer synthetic customer value tiers, uncover high-traction organic geographic markets, audit promotional discount cannibalization, and formulate a targeted promo sunsetting playbook.

---

## 2. Actual Technical & Analytical Execution
- **Feature Engineering Pipeline**: Engineered 16 customer value features on 3,900 customers using Python and SQL, categorizing the customer base into four value tiers: *Diamond*, *Gold*, *Silver*, and *Bronze*.
- **Geographic Spend-Promo Matrix**: Developed 5 structured SQL segmentation queries and mapped spend vs. promo dependency across all 50 US states, identifying regional markets with high organic demand versus discount-driven sales.
- **Discount Over-Subsidization Audit**: Identified that **42.9% of top-tier (Diamond/Gold) customers** were receiving unnecessary promotional discounts despite exhibiting high organic repeat purchase intent.
- **Founder Decision Dashboard & Playbook**: Designed an executive dashboard and promotional sunset playbook projected to recover **$20,000+ in annual gross margin** without impacting customer retention.
